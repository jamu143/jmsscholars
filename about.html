About

Dr James AlexBSc, MSc, MPhil, PhD

Mathematician, graph theorist, and educator with over a decade of experience teaching mathematics at university level across Perth and Bangalore.

James holds a PhD in Mathematics from Curtin University, where his research sits in structural graph theory. Alongside his academic and research work, he has spent years developing exam-focused mathematics curricula for WACE students, drawing on the same rigour he brings to peer-reviewed research.

"Mathematics is learned the way it's practised — one careful step at a time, checked against real problems, not shortcuts."

The gap between knowing the material and performing under exam conditions is where marks are won or lost. That gap is what JMS Scholars is built to close — structured WACE tutoring drawn from a real past-paper bank, not generic worksheets.
