Getting started

How do I enrol my student?

Start with a free consult through the contact page. We'll talk through your student's current level and where they sit in the relevant WACE course sequence, then confirm a weekly session time.

What year levels do you tutor?

JMS Scholars focuses specifically on WACE Year 11 and 12 Mathematics — Specialist, Methods, and Applications.

Can my student join partway through the year?

Yes. The 40-week programme is sequenced by term, so new students are placed at the appropriate point in the current term's topics rather than starting from week one.

The curriculum

Where do the worksheets come from?

Worksheets are drawn from a curated bank of real WACE past papers.

What's the difference between calculator-free and calculator-assumed?

These mirror the two papers in the actual WACE exams — Specialist, Methods, and Applications are all structured this way. Every worksheet is tagged accordingly, so students build fluency in both — not just the one they find easier.

How is progress tracked?

Through the student portal — completed worksheets, upcoming topics, and session history are all visible in one dashboard once you're logged in.

The student portal

What can students access after logging in?

Worksheets and worked solutions organised by week, a progress dashboard, and discussion boards for each topic where students can ask questions and see answers from previous sessions.

Is the discussion board moderated?

Yes. All discussion activity is visible to and moderated directly, with no private unmonitored channels between students.

Can parents see progress too?

A read-only progress summary can be shared with parents on request — reach out directly to set this up.
