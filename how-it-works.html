01

Free consult

Every student starts with a short, no-obligation conversation. We talk through current grades, target ATAR, and which course — Specialist, Methods, or Applications — actually fits. No assumptions made in advance.

02

Placement in the sequence

Rather than starting from scratch, students join the sequenced curriculum at the point matching their current term — so no time is lost re-teaching material they've already covered in class.

03

Weekly sessions and worksheets

Regular sessions are paired with take-home worksheets drawn from the WACE past-paper bank, tagged Calculator-free or Calculator-assumed to match the real exam structure. Progress is tracked week by week.

04

Exam-ready by term's end

In the weeks leading into WACE exams, sessions shift to full timed practice papers and worked solutions — building the exact fluency needed for both the Calculator-free and Calculator-assumed papers.
